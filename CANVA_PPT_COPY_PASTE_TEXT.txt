SLIDE 2: DETAILED EXPLANATION, PROBLEM ADDRESSED & INNOVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RailBlock-AI: Smart Railway Block Planning & Dispatch Engine

Detailed Explanation
• AI-powered master coordinator for Indian Railways track maintenance & train dispatching.
• Combines P-Way (Tracks), TRD (Electrification), and S&T (Signals) block requests into single joint maintenance windows.
• Features real-time AI train dispatching and automated loop-line siding to prevent domino delays.

How it Addresses the Problem
• Reduces 3 to 4 separate daily track closures per section into 1 consolidated block.
• Holds slow freight trains on loop lines, isolating delays to single trains.
• Recovers 42.9% of lost track capacity and saves 6+ hours of train detention daily.

Innovation & Uniqueness
• Constraint-Based Joint Window Optimizer (Google OR-Tools MILP).
• Dynamic Overtake & Speed Profile Predictor for Indian Railways tracks.
• "Where Is My Track" style live station-by-station visualization dashboard.

SLIDE 4: FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Feasibility Analysis
• 100% Software Solution: Requires zero physical hardware modifications to tracks or trains.
• Scalable Architecture: Easily deployable across all 68 Indian Railway divisions.
• High Performance Engine: Runs complex constraint solvers in seconds on standard server hardware.

Challenges & Risks
• High operational complexity across multi-department workflows.
• Varying section speeds and signal block lengths.
• Resistance to moving from manual BDMS scheduling to automated AI directives.

Strategies to Overcome
• Seamless CRIS Integration: Connects directly with CRIS (COA & BDMS) APIs.
• Human-in-the-Loop Approval: Section Controllers and Station Masters retain final click approval.
• Phased Corridors Rollout: Starting with high-density trunk routes (e.g. Delhi-Mathura line).
